Privacy notice

Last updated {{ '2026-10-13'|d }}

This notice explains what personal data LocumDoc ("we") collects through locumdoc.me, why, and the choices you have. We process data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016).

What we collect

  • Doctors: name, contact details, nationality, country of residence, specialty, grade, qualifications, licence details, licensing status, availability, languages, and the documents you upload (CV, licences, certificates, passport if you choose to add it).
  • Facilities: organisation details and the name, role, email and phone of the account holder.
  • Everyone: login details (your password is stored only as a secure hash), and basic technical logs such as IP address and time of requests, kept for security.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and match doctors with locum jobs (performance of a contract).
  • To share a doctor's profile and documents with facilities the doctor applies to, and, if the doctor allows it, with verified facilities searching the directory (consent, which you can withdraw in your profile at any time).
  • To verify facilities and keep the platform safe (legitimate interest).
  • To send emails about your account, applications and invitations. We don't send marketing without asking.

Who can see your data

Doctor documents are never public. Facilities see a doctor's profile and documents only when that doctor has applied to one of their jobs, or when the doctor has chosen to appear in the directory and the facility has been verified. Our administrators can see all accounts to verify and support them.

These providers process data on our behalf, under written data processing terms:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): stores account records in Cloud Firestore and uploaded documents in Cloud Storage, in the European Union, and provides analytics if you allow it.
  • Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication): if you choose Continue with Google, Google confirms your identity and shares your name and email address with us. The log-in and sign-up pages load Google's sign-in service so the button works.
  • Render: runs the website's servers, in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Our email provider: delivers account and application emails.

International transfers

Because placements are in the Gulf, profiles you share with facilities are transferred to those facilities in Qatar (and, later, other Gulf countries). We only make this transfer when you apply or allow directory visibility.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile and every uploaded file are deleted immediately. Records of applications already shared with a facility may be retained by that facility under its own obligations.

Your rights

You can access, correct, download or delete your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself from your profile and account pages. For anything else, email {{ contact_email }}. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies make the site work and don't need your permission: one keeps you logged in and protects forms from forgery, and one remembers your cookie choice.

With your permission only, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the site is used, for example which pages people visit, which device and browser they use, their approximate location (country or city), and when key steps such as signing up or applying happen. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_* for six months. Analytics never receives your name, email, profile details or documents, Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off, and Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses. Google may process this data outside the EU, including in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU's standard contractual clauses. The legal basis is your consent.

You can change your choice at any time in Cookie settings, linked at the bottom of every page. Fonts are served from our own server, and we don't use advertising cookies.
