How it works for facilities

Licensing in Qatar, in plain terms

Doctors practise in Qatar under a licence from the Department of Healthcare Professions (DHP) at the Ministry of Public Health. Your profile records where you are in this process, so facilities can plan start dates realistically.

Many placements come with licensing support from the facility.

Read the full Qatar guide
  1. Primary source verificationA DHP-approved verification company confirms your degree, licences and experience with the issuing bodies.
  2. DHP evaluationDHP reviews your file against its qualification categories. Category 1 and 2 specialty qualifications don't need the qualifying exam.
  3. Qualifying exam, if requiredNeeded for some qualifications. Recognised exams such as PLAB 1 or USMLE Step 2 can exempt general practitioners.
  4. Licence for the roleLocum licences cover a permanent physician for up to six months. Temporary licences last six months while a full licence is processed.

Summary based on DHP's published guidelines for physicians. Requirements change; DHP's current rules always apply.
