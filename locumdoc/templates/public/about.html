About LocumDoc

A locum marketplace for doctors and healthcare facilities in the Gulf.

Gulf hospitals and clinics rely on locum doctors to cover leave, seasonal demand and new services. Finding the right doctor, with the right qualifications and a realistic route to a local licence, usually means agencies, inboxes full of CVs and weeks of back and forth.

LocumDoc puts both sides in one place. Doctors describe what they can cover, when they're free and where they stand with licensing. Facilities post what they need and search for who fits. Everyone sees the same information, and every status change is shared.

Where we work

We're starting with Qatar, where every physician is licensed by the Department of Healthcare Professions. The UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain follow.

How we keep it trustworthy

  • Every facility is verified before its jobs appear.
  • Doctors' documents are private files, never public links. Only facilities they apply to, or that they allow to search, can open them.
  • Doctors can delete their account and every file at any time.
