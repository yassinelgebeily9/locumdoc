Working as a locum doctor in Qatar

What to expect from licensing, documents and placements, based on the published guidelines of the Department of Healthcare Professions.

Create a doctor profile See jobs in Qatar

Who licenses doctors

All physicians in Qatar are licensed by the {{ country.licensing_body }}. Facilities can't employ a doctor, even for a short locum, until DHP has issued the right licence for the role.

The pathway

  1. Primary source verification (PSV)A DHP-approved verification company checks your degree, specialty certificates, licences and work experience directly with the bodies that issued them. Start this early; it is usually the slowest step.
  2. Registration and evaluationYou apply online with your documents. DHP aims to review a complete file in about 25 working days, and a preliminary evaluation stays valid for six months. You may be invited to an interview.
  3. Qualifying exam, where requiredSpecialists with category 1 or 2 qualifications don't sit the exam; category 3 qualifications do. General practitioners can be exempted by exams such as IFOM CSE (score 60+), USMLE Step 2, PLAB 1, MCCQE 1 or the AMC MCQ.
  4. LicensingOnce eligible, the licence application is reviewed in about 15 working days. It's linked to the facility you'll work at.

Licence types for short placements

  • Locum: covers a permanent physician in a government or semi-government facility for up to six months. The facility provides a letter of intent and an undertaking from its medical director.
  • Temporary: valid for six months and not renewable, reviewed in about five working days. You must apply for the full licence within that period.
  • Non-resident: for repeated visits under a named supervising physician, with at least five years of post-degree practice.
  • Visitor or short notice: for specialists with ten or more years' experience. Each visit is limited to two weeks at one facility.

Documents to have ready

  • Passport, and your Qatar ID if you have one
  • Recent photo and a CV in DHP's template
  • Degree and specialty certificates with official transcripts
  • Experience certificates covering the last five years
  • Every medical licence held during that experience
  • Your PSV report and a certificate of good standing
  • For licensing: medical report, recognised CPR certificate, and police clearance

You can upload all of these to your LocumDoc profile. Facilities only see them when you apply, or if you choose to appear in the doctor directory.
