Built around DHP licensing

The longest part of bringing a locum to Qatar is the licence. LocumDoc asks doctors for the details that decide how quickly they can start, so you can shortlist people who are ready.

  1. Locum licence for government and semi-government rolesCovers a permanent physician for up to six months, with a letter of intent and an undertaking from your medical director.
  2. Temporary licenceSix months, non-renewable, while the full licence is processed.
  3. Visiting and short-noticeFor senior specialists on short visits. Each visit is limited to two weeks at one facility.
